Service Details

These notes explain why each category matters when reviewing cloud costs.

Why track compute costs?

Compute services are often one of the largest contributors to cloud spending. Monitoring idle instances, oversized virtual machines, and unexpected scaling events helps teams avoid unnecessary monthly costs.

Why track storage costs?

Storage usage tends to grow gradually over time. Old backups, duplicate files, and unused datasets can increase costs without being noticed.

Why monitor network costs?

Data transfer charges, content delivery traffic, and external requests can add unexpected costs. Monitoring network activity helps identify usage spikes early.
